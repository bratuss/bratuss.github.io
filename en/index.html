Applications

What can you make with a CNC router?

CNC routers are an essential part of modern manufacturing. Thanks to excellent precision (±0.01 mm) and material versatility, a CNC router can process wood, plywood, MDF, acrylic, aluminum and much more. Our clients in Latvia are already successfully using Wattsan machines for the following applications:

  • Mēbeļu ražošana: durvis, rāmji, rokturi, kāpnes, virtuves fasādes un interjera elementi
  • Reklāmas industrija: logotipi, izkārtnes, apjomīgi burti, LED kastes un dekoratīvi paneļi
  • Suvenīri un dāvanas: gravētas plāksnes, rotaļlietas, personalizēti izstrādājumi
  • Tehniskās detaļas: blīves, organizatori, ieliktņi, detaļas mehānismiem
  • Būvniecība: kāpņu pakāpieni, margas, dekoratīvie fasādes elementi
Selection Guide

How to choose the right CNC router?

Based on our 20+ years of equipment consulting experience, we recommend answering these two key questions before purchasing:

  • What products will I manufacture and from what materials?The material type (wood, MDF, plywood, acrylic, aluminum) determines the required spindle power and tools. For example, aluminum processing requires a more powerful spindle and cooling system.
  • What is the planned production volume and required speed?For hobbies and small orders, the A1 series will suffice. For professional production with heavy workloads, we recommend the M1 or M3 series — they are designed for 8-12 hours of daily operation.

Piemēram, durvju ražošanai ideāli piemēroti būs 1325 sērijas modeļi (darba laukums 1300×2500 mm), kas atbilst standarta durvju sagatavēm. Savukārt hobijiem un nelieliem suvenīriem pilnīgi pietiks ar kompaktiem modeļiem kā 0404 Mini, kuri, neskatoties uz savu izmēru, ir aprīkoti ar jaudīgām vārpstām.

Which CNC router should a beginner choose?
For beginners, we recommend the Wattsan 0404 MINI or 0609 MINI models — they are compact, easy to use and equipped with an intuitive control system. These models are ideal for hobbies, educational purposes and small-scale product manufacturing. After mastering the basics, you can easily transition to larger models.
